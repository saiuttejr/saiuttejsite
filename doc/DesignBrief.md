# Design Brief — `saiuttej.com`

**Sai Uttej Rajoju**  
*Software Engineer · Hyderabad, India*

---

## Core Philosophy: Express Rather Than Tell

This website is not a corporate stat sheet, a SaaS template of boxed cards, or a resume pasted into HTML. It is designed as an **unhurried editorial walkthrough**—closer to opening a well-art-directed magazine issue than scrolling a template portfolio.

1. **First-person, human voice:** Short, natural sentences (`"Hey, I’m Sai."`). No third-person biography, no buzzword walls, no cringey captions under photographs explaining what the picture already shows.
2. **Open canvas, zero card clutter:** Content sits directly on a warm, breathable paper ground separated by proportion, typography, and subtle hairline dividers—never inside rows of pastel boxes or pill badges.
3. **Two distinct worlds connected by a live morph:**
   - **Work (`/`) — Warm Ivory (`#fafbfe`) & Electric Cobalt (`#1447e6`)**: Calm, structured, typographically led. Walks the visitor through four beats:
     1. **Scroll-Parting Editorial Cover:** Full-screen opening with `Hey, I’m Sai.` and rounded personal photography (`sai-pergola.jpg` and `sai-palace.jpg`) that gently parts left and right on scroll.
     2. **01 — What I do:** A single statement on day-to-day engineering at MAQ Software in Hyderabad, paired with an open two-column baseline for Experience and verified Microsoft credentials (`AI-103`, `AI-200`, `AB-620`).
     3. **02 — Selected work:** All four shipped projects (`I. Eatensity`, `II. FigureTools`, `III. FinPulse AI`, `IV. Kanso`) visible at once in an open typographic index with direct live and GitHub links.
     4. **03 — Say hi:** Direct email (`saiuttej.r@gmail.com`), `Resume PDF`, `GitHub`, `LinkedIn`, and a prompt to step into `Space`.
   - **Space (`/space`) — One rooftop, golden hour (`#6a3d45`) to night (`#05070f`)**: Flipping the persistent top-right toggle drops the visitor onto a rooftop terrace at golden hour. The room is one fixed illustration and scrolling is the evening passing: the only copy is the name and a large "Space" in the sky, no captions, nothing that explains itself. The sky is never empty: evening clouds that cool as it darkens, birds already crossing when the page opens, and sky lanterns that glow brighter at night.
     1. **Photos of Sai:** Four small overlapping cards in the empty sky of the hero. Whole frames, never cropped or zoomed, and no staged portraits.
     2. **The wall:** Rounded tiles of photographs, a Watching tile and a Reading tile with cover art, and a Lichess tile. Photos are chosen for variety of colour and place. At night the wall dims except around a lamp that follows the pointer.
     3. **Links as windows:** `Letterboxd`, `Lichess`, `MyAnimeList`, `Goodreads`, and email are lit windows in a building that stands on the terrace, each with a small illustrated room. Real interests, expressed rather than listed, with no mini-games or stat walls.
     4. **Small things that react:** A chess table, a painter, and a car respond to clicks and to scroll speed. The cursor itself is a small F1 car, an echo of the old site: it turns toward where it is going, leaves fading tyre marks that deepen with speed and sharp turns, and turns its headlights on at dusk. Mouse users only. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the cursor stays a car but goes calm (short ease, quick turning, shorter and softer tyre marks) and everything else is frozen.
