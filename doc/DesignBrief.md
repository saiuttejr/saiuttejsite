# Design Brief — `saiuttej.com`

**Sai Uttej Rajoju**  
*Software Engineer · Hyderabad, India*

---

## Core Philosophy: Express Rather Than Tell

This website is not a corporate stat sheet, a SaaS template of boxed cards, or a resume pasted into HTML. It is designed as an **unhurried editorial walkthrough**—closer to opening a well-art-directed magazine issue than scrolling a template portfolio.

1. **First-person, human voice:** Short, natural sentences (`"Hey, I’m Sai."`). No third-person biography, no buzzword walls, no cringey captions under photographs explaining what the picture already shows.
2. **Open canvas, zero card clutter:** Content sits directly on a warm, breathable paper ground separated by proportion, typography, and subtle hairline dividers—never inside rows of pastel boxes or pill badges.
3. **Two distinct worlds connected by a live morph:**
   - **Work (`/`) — Warm Ivory (`#fafbfe`) & Electric Cobalt (`#1447e6`)**: Calm, structured, typographically led. Walks the visitor through four beats:
     1. **Scroll-Parting Editorial Cover:** Full-screen opening with `Hey, I’m Sai.` and rounded personal photography (`sai-pergola.jpg` and `sai-palace.jpg`) that gently parts left and right on scroll.
     2. **01 — What I do:** A single statement on day-to-day engineering at MAQ Software in Hyderabad, paired with an open two-column baseline for Experience and verified Microsoft credentials (`AI-103`, `AI-200`, `AB-620`).
     3. **02 — Selected work:** All four shipped projects (`I. Eatensity`, `II. FigureTools`, `III. FinPulse AI`, `IV. Kanso`) visible at once in an open typographic index with direct live and GitHub links.
     4. **03 — Say hi:** Direct email (`saiuttej.r@gmail.com`), `Resume PDF`, `GitHub`, `LinkedIn`, and a prompt to flip into `After Hours`.
   - **After Hours (`/space`) — Warm Paper (`#fdf8f4`) & Persimmon (`#e03516`)**: Flipping the persistent top-right toggle smoothly morphs the palette, shifts the display voice to `Sentient Italic`, and opens a separate off-the-clock world:
     1. **Opening Spread:** *“Off the clock.”* paired with rounded photography (`sai-palace.jpg`).
     2. **Interactive Play:** A frameless **Formula 1 five-red-lights reflex test** (`I — Race Sunday`) and a playable **4×4 mate-in-one chess board** (`II — Endgame Corner`).
     3. **Quiet Footer:** Direct links out to `Letterboxd`, `MyAnimeList`, `Goodreads`, and email—without turning personal reading or watchlists into a show-off wall.
